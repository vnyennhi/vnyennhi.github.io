I am not a Christian

But I am praying

For your help and for your patience

Please God

Rescue these hollow spirits at ease

I am praying for her

For her loss and for her fear

Protect her peace in the clear

Please God

She shall not be one with tears

I am praying for him

For his path and for his mean

Watching him closely with no blink

Please God

He shall not be one with sins

I am praying for her

For her deeds and for her mind

Keeping her soul off the tie

Please God

She shall not be one with cries

I am praying for him

For his health and for his choice

Saving him wholly with the poise

Please God

He shall not be one with spoils

Might I be greed or be in need

I am willing to be punished

I would take a ton of rock or biff

For a wink of smile on their lip

Please God

Accept those sincere prays

Though you rejects my soul is okay

I am in maze all these days

Try to get out while still may

Somehow cannot shed a tear

Somehow cannot leak a cry

Forgive and forget

================================

This is for some friends of mine. Loving them is not enough. Praying for them is not enough. Hope might be the only way left ...
