The open sky
No road no path and no line
With wings to fly
Any place any high
Freely
As long as you might


\_ Angel, oh my beloved Angel
Where will you head?
Where would you go?
When cloud is dead?
When wind is slow?


\_ Little pixie, my little dear
I’m in a rush
Did you not hear?
Words from the seers
There would be 
And there will be chaos with tears 
Demon is near and all is feared


\_ Angel, oh my good Angel
What should I do?
What could I do?
To help you out against the foe
From now and on let me with you
Redeem the sin
Please take my wings
Please take my will
For you and for real


\_ Little pixie, my little honey
You are so sweet and so naïve
See that demon killing humans
Blood’s all around and there is no doubt
He is devil and that are sins
What should I do?
What could I do?
Come praying with me, little pixie
For all the minds to be at ease
For all the souls to rest in peace
For what will be


\_ Angel, oh my kind Angel
Do you realize, don’t you realize?
What you just do, what you just don’t
With siren voice you pray for him
Erase his deeds, wipe out his sins
Punish him not but grant him wings
Angel’s wings
Angel’s white wings
What should you do
What did you do?
From now and on you have no wing
From now and on you become him


\_ Little pixie, my little darling
I’m in a crush
Did you not see?
Demon is bad and that is he
But you’re not him and I’m not him
For all he did we could not meet
And all he killed are all devils
So it’s okay to be this way
From now and on no longer may
A good indeed be harmed in deep


\_ Angel, oh my my 
From now and on you are demon
What should I call?
What could I call?
For all known that are now at fall
I do not understand
I could not comprehend
Why did so, why did those?
Tell me demon what I should have known


\_ Little pixie, my little love
That is a guilt you shall not know
The why is harsh as the crash is crap
Wanting to know
Find out yourself
See that baby, kill her for me\!
The answer is let it be


\_ Demon, oh my my demon
I did what be told 
I did what should not be done
The child has slept in heaven bed
But where are my wings and where is my dress
Why on the ground stand both my feet
What should I do?
What did I do?


\_ Little human, my little one
What goes around has come around
You kill a child and take her place
From now and on you are a man
A deeply sinful one
Like all others in human race
For now is the time I do my job
Finish you off and rot away
First act as a demon and last as an angel
For me to completely be him.


Farewell pixie, my little pixie


The open sky
No road no path and no line
Without wings 
Still fly
Any place any high
Freely
As long as you might
