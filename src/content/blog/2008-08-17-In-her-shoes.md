“Ring…”
The alarm clock strikes at 4 a.m. Through the window portière, the crescent moon sheds light on the messy room. Kai gets up automatically. Like a robot, he rushes to the bathroom and makes a quick morning wash up. Opening the wardrobe, Kai desperately searches for his driver's uniform until he suddenly remembers it is vacation days, and all his clothes are at the laundry right now. All over the floor are confetti, pizza, glasses of wine … the remnants from last night's party, not to mention his headache.
Kai has been a truck driver for many years. He delivers furniture and household appliances for supermarket systems nationwide. His job makes him travel a lot. Hardly has he got for himself a day at home alone. That’s why he’s still a single man. Lounging comfortably on the sofa, Kai wonders what he’s going to do for the next work\-free 24 hours. No\! Just 23 left. Kai really hates wasting his time thinking that much about things. He can use the whole day sleeping and watching movies. But that will make him feel tired on the next delivery schedule. He decides to go jogging instead.
After a long time with no use, Kai’s sneakers are covered in a thick layer of dust. But he doesn’t mind. He just pulls the shoes out of the cabinet, makes some sloppy knots, and leaves in an excited mood. How long has he been separated from this beautiful world? Every day his truck speeds along these streets, but little does he know how blossomy the roadside daisies are. Now, without the glass window, Kai can, not only breathe the fresh air but also hear the morning birdsong by himself. Lovely, warm, and cheerful\!
When the very first thread of silky sunlight shines above the sluggish clouds, Kai returns home, feeling better than ever. He removes his sneakers and put them in the old position without taking a look. However, suddenly, the sneakers fall out of the cabinet which is occupied already. Surprisingly, Kai pulls out of the small dark locker, where he has pulled the sneakers out just an hour ago, a pair of beautiful high\-heeled shoes, woman high heels.
It takes Kai such a long pause to remember the shoes and its owner as well. The high heels were a birthday present for his girlfriend, maybe an ex\-girlfriend by now.
\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-
3 years ago …
It was a beautiful morning. Kai woke up early to pick up his Emi for her school. It had been a year of dating and every day was scheduled. He picked her up at 7, then they met for lunch at 12 noon. After school, if she didn’t have homework or he didn’t have to work late, they went to see a movie, and a late dinner sometimes.
They were walking down the Shibuya, a very crowded shopping town in Tokyo.
\_ Hey Emi, do you want something to drink?
\_ Oh, I’m thirsty here. Some ice tea may help. You’re so kind\!
Kai came back as fast as he could after a very long queue. He smiled happily handling the cup to Emi, but she was looking through a glass window of a luxurious shoe store. There was a pair of appealing swanky high heels with glossy leather sparkling all the way through the crystal glass. The price tag appeared clearly under the light. 300$\!
\_ Kai thanks you\! The tea really makes me feel good. Let’s go\!
Emi took the cup from Kai somehow and walked fast before he could open his mouth about the shoes. She must love those high heels. However, smiling inside, he realized that his Emi didn’t want to bother him about such a thing.
\_ Do you love those shoes?
\_ Yes, it’s wonderful\! You should have come back earlier to see how those shoes fit me.
\_ Why do women always go crazy about shoes?
\_ Because inside a woman lies a little girl. That little princess always believes in fairy tales. Those high heels make us feel like a Cinderella waiting for the prince to come. And a princess always wears the most beautiful shoes in the ball.
That night Kai thought a lot. He was just a blue\-collar worker with average pay. More importantly, he was saving up for marriage, a marriage that he had never asked Emi. He wanted the wedding to be magnificent and splendid, and she would be the most gorgeous bride ever. But think of Emi’s look and those luxurious shoes, Kai decided to do something different from the big plan.
…/…/…
Emi’s birthday is today. Kai asked the boss for a day off. He waked up early in the morning and ran off to the shoe store. After days and nights of hard work and cut down on living expenses, Kai could finally afford the high heels in his dream or his future wife’s dream. Having the shoes wrapped beautifully, Kai went to Emi’s house in an excited mood. He was going to do something had never been done before: A proposal, not with a diamond ring, but a pair of high heels.
To Kai’s surprise, her house was such a mess with carton boxes, suitcases, packages everywhere. Emi was flabbergasted when seeing Kai in front of her. She didn’t expect him to come, especially in such a situation. The motion in his eyes told her that everything would be hard for the time had come.
\_ You will go abroad to study? Why now? Why didn’t you tell me about this?
\_ I wanted to, but I’m afraid that you would be upset about this.
\_ How many years … How long will you go?
\_ I don’t know. My father wants me to stay with him there.
\_ Look\! Those shoes, the shoes at the store, I buy it for your birthday.
\_ I’m really sorry. I love you, but maybe this life, we don’t mean for each other.
Emi took the shoes, with all the strength she had, she broke the heels. Then, she threw the high heels out of her door. She threw him out of her life. “Don’t mean for each other? Did she mean the shoes? Or him? He took a look back at her eyes. Tears were rolling down, smearing all over her face. Sadness blossomed into a cry, where the slender body’s shaking by the heart\-quake. It’s over\!
…/…/…
Days after that day, Kai woke up early without having to go to work. He had moved his job from the factory to the road. Days and nights, just Kai and his truck, along the road they went, wiped all the sad memories from his past. But today, he wanted to face it once again. He took the high heels to the store, asked the shoemaker to repair them.
\_ Those shoes are very well designed, very well made, but very badly broken. Why you want them fixed?
\_ They’re something very important to me, even when I can’t use it any longer.
\_ It’s hard for me, you know, such damage\!
\_ But you must be responsible for everything you make. I will pay any price.
\_ Money doesn’t buy you things. Especially shoes\! People don’t buy shoes themselves. Those shoes choose their owners. The destiny of a pair of shoes or high heels is to find their one and only owner. Between their soul and mind is a race toward this owner’s heart, to stick with that person until they become broken. These high heels are broken, so do their soul and mind, cannot be repaired.
\_ … Thank you\! I take those back.
As Kai just turned his back around, stepped forward the shoemaker continued his story without handling Kai the shoes.
\_ A relationship is also like this. Once broken, never be the same. A hug, a kiss … maybe a bandage to a wounded heart. But not for long 'till it bleeds again one day.
\_ What do you mean?
\_ I can make a bandage for those shoes. Another girl can use them. But they’ll be broken again soon. Why don’t just throw them away and moving forward with a new pair?
\_ Please fix it for me. There will be no other girl.
\_ As you want\!
Returning from the shoe store, Kai put away those high heels and forgot everything. The girl, the high heels, the shoemaker’s story … Just like a dream\!
Until today …
Keep staring at the high heels, Kai tries his best to remember about their owner. But little does he know that deep inside his heart is a locked room for Emi. Now, he can’t detect her face, her voice, or her smile with his human brain anymore. The heartbreak leaves him a gap in the middle of his lifetime, within a blank he has to fill out. The shoes have been repaired carefully. It’s difficult to tell that they have been broken before. They just look like a pair of perfect new high heels at the store.
Kai stands up. The shoes on the floor are still twinkling at his eyes. He thinks for a moment he can see through them, see their soul in his lover’s eyes that day. Suddenly, Kai puts his feet into the high heels and stands on it. Like a miracle, the shoes fit him really well, though he has bigger feet than Emi. Being hypnotized by the shoes and his twisted mind, Kai just walks out of his house on those high heels ridiculously. He completely loses control of himself. Not Kai or his feet makes these moves but the shoes lead the way themselves.
One step, two steps … Kai and the high heels continue their moves on the street with many whispers behind their back. Kai doesn’t care about that much. He does not give a damn about those people anyway, and neighbors are just strangers to him. He totally focuses on his walk with the shoes. Deep down inside those high heels, Kai thinks he finds a very warm feeling of love. He believes the shoes will lead him to their rightful owner, to his lover. One street, two streets … a long way behind and a long way ahead, Kai suddenly finds himself standing at the bus terminal, all alone with the high heels.
…/…/…
\_ Kai, I’m so tired. Why don’t we take the bus there?
\_ Don’t be that extravagant. The park is just 2 stops from here. Moreover, walking is good for your health too.
\_ But not with the high heels. It’s killing my leg, you know.
\_ I told you not to wear those. High heels? Like High hell\! You should use sandals or sneakers more often, instead of these.
\_ High heels are much prettier. Anyway, I can’t walk any longer. Will you carry me on your back?
\_ In public? You’re not a baby.
\_ Ah, but you said that I’m your baby before.
\_ That’s when you behave more reasonably.
\_ Just forget it. I hate you\!
…/…/…
Now in her shoes, in her high heels, Kai can feel how painful his ankle is. She must have been this hurt all the time wearing those. Why couldn’t he be more compassionate of her? How regretful he is now don’t help him bring back his Emi. Kai is just standing there, on the high heels at the bus terminal. Buses come and go. People come and go. They stare at him and the woman's shoes he is wearing. Not even a moment he notices about things happening around him. A long silence inside his mind tries to awake the sleeping memories of Emi. 
Unexpectedly, the high heels move and jump on the bus no.73, the bus to central park. Everyone on the bus look at him like an insane person. Some whispers start while he is walking to the end of the bus. Choosing a quiet window seat, Kai stares into space outside the window. His eyes somehow catch sight of a couple. That happy couple is sharing a hotdog bun. They are smiling freely, and in their eyes is only the image of each other. Emi likes hotdogs. Kai remembers that. His mind is empty again, while the view of the road keeps blurring in his teary eyes.
The bus has stopped at the central park. It’s already 9. Most people have gone to work. Kai still sits on the bus for a little while after everyone gets off. He doesn’t know why, but he feels dizzy about everything today. How come he appears in public with such a pair of high heels? How come he forgets almost everything about Emi, his only true heart? How come …? 
Kai doesn’t have much time for hesitating. The shoes make him continue their journey. Fast, faster, and faster, Kai is running on high heels. “No\! Stop this\! Or you will be broken once more time\!” The shoes don’t listen to Kai. They go on their own way without caring about the difficulties that Kai has to follow them left and right. After a long chasing pavement to the park pond, the high heels stop by the rocky stairs. Kai falls down on those stairs, feeling deeply hurt as his feet have burnt to the bone. What’s a mistake\! What do those shoes want? Where would this journey end? 
Hard breathing after running on the high heels, Kai decides to take a short break here before that crazy things force him to go again. Unexpectedly, Kai sees a young boy walking on a pair of woman shoes. He smiles inside at that view. Maybe that boy is the same as him. He’s not the only one who is stuck in such a situation. The pleasure doesn’t last for long. A girl runs after the boy and chases him down right in front of his eyes. She is wearing a pair of big men's sandals. They just exchange their shoes for a game. How sad Kai is to find out what is going on\!
The young couple plays and laughs happily there. They’re carefree, joyful, and … so real. Why can’t that be him and Emi? Many questions for a day make him feel sorrow. Naturally, a teardrop falls down from his eyes, warm, quiet, and lonely. That tear he must have held on for such a long time. It’s still very sad and hurt. Why didn’t he cry that day? Why didn’t he do anything to hold on to Emi? Kai stands up very quickly and starts to run again. He runs very fast without seeing the road through tears, without hearing the yell of people, without thinking how painful his leg would be. And …
“Crack\!”
The heels are broken, once again. 
Lying by the park pond, with his face up to the blue sky, Kai laughs his heart out loud. He has been hiding his feeling by working for those times have gone. The heart is still bleeding when the heels break again. Now left on the ground are the high heels and his broken heart. He has to get that fixed once again. Both the shoes and his heart\!
Walking on his bare foot, Kai goes to that old shoe store determinately. He decides to give those high heels back to that shoemaker, and buy a new pair, maybe leather sandals. Kai is here, at that glass window. The place where those high heels were now laying a pair of new fashionable boots. A very charming lady is trying on those boots. As soon as she smiles with the new boots on her feet, Kai remembers exactly Emi’s smile. 
When she lifts her face, a sudden silence falls at both sides of the window. A big surprise.
That’s Emi\!
…/…/…
Has he forgotten how beautiful Emi is? Her face has always shined like the moon and her eyes are sparkling stars. Her curly hair falls perfectly on such a slim slender body. And yet her smile, like a diamond in the sky, is the most precious treasure in this world. Taking a deep breath in, Kai walks into the store, ignoring Emi as if they are total strangers, and talks to the shoemaker:\_ Please help me fix this pair one more time?\_ Again? How are they broken so badly now?\_ I try to walk on these high heels, but they do not fit me. What was I thinking? I should have known that I cannot be in her shoes. We are not meant for each other\! Not this time.\_ Ah, so will you exchange for a new pair or what?\_ Just repair and give them to their rightful owner, if she still wants those. \_ How about you?\_ Normal person like me, any sloppy sandals or sneakers will do. Never mind\!
As Kai about to leave, Emi suddenly raises her voice:\_ Such a nice pair of heels\! What is made with high quality would always be in good condition, no matter how hard times tore them apart. It would be a waste to throw things this good away, especially when the heart and soul are involved.On barefoot, Kai leaves the shoe store, giving Emi cold shoulders. The pale look on her face could tell a lot about how different things are now. What does Emi mean by saying those heart\-aching words? Neither one was wrong on the breakup. No promises should be kept after years of loss and salvation. This would be the best for them, right? Both us and that pair of high heels …
A long sleep helps his feet back to the ground. Yesterday was like a dream, except for the fact that the secret chamber of his heart had been opened once again. It’s a good thing today is a workday. One more time he would spend all his time die hard working to erase all the traces of Emi. This time would be for good\! With such positive thoughts, he hits the road earlier than expected. Pink’s greatest song is airing on the radio. In so good a mood he is, Kai sings along to “Just give me a reason” without any further thinking about stuff. Maybe he could finish work early and then enjoy some drinks at a local bar. 
The first house on the schedule is just small delivery with some decoration lamps. The second one, on the other hand, has purchased a full set of furniture for a whole apartment. The owners might be moving in from a far place so they have to buy all new furniture. He has rung the bell twice, but no luck. While Kai is searching for a contact number, Emi opens the door and greets him with her usual friendly smile. She acts naturally as if she is expecting him, her ex\-boyfriend.
In the complete hush, Kai and Emi move in all the furniture in a surprisingly short time. Maybe the lover chemistry helps them understand each other’s move to such an extent that no word needs to be exchanged. Once or twice their eyes meet, a little blush on Emi’s cheek and a little rush his face turns away. What is this called? Love? Affection? Bond? He does not know, but this feeling is so good that he actually regrets finishing the work so soon, leaving this peaceful and tranquil atmosphere so fast.
After getting on his truck, Kai suddenly sees in the side mirror, Emi is running after him. She is running on the red high heels, the pair of shoes has been perfectly repaired as if it has been damaged twice. Through the truck window, Emi hands over a big box wrapped in glossy red paper. “Please help me deliver this to the owner\!”. Leaving no time for Kai to say yes or no, she returns to her house in just a blink of an eye. Emi has always been fast on heels.
Take a closer look at the box, Kai notices a small tag attached. On the lavender\-smelt paper lies the beautiful handwriting in which is his home address. This is his present\! Kai stops for a second as his heart skip a beat reading that. Slowly he opens the box, then finds a pair of sporty sneakers with green strings and dots. He bursts out laughing. How come after years can Emi still remember everything from his address to his favorite color and even his shoe size? What did she go to study abroad for anyway? Has he always been this careless and Emi been this thoughtful? Seem like he owns both Emi and those red high heels an apology. Not Emi but he is the one who breaks them apart.
It's in the stars It's been written in the scars on our hearts That we're not broken just bent And we can learn to love again
Put the sneakers on, Kai quickly stomps the pedal. Only 2 more packages to be delivered and he can have the whole evening free. He can finally enjoy some drinks at a local bar. With Emi. And in her shoes. 
The End.
