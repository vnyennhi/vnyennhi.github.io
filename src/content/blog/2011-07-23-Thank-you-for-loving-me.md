I used to believe that I could write more once fall in love. However, back in the happy days, I did not write much about you and about us. I was busy being with you, staying in your arms, and loving you, so I have no time for thinking, no time for writing.

Now when it's all over, I really want to write you a thank you note for everything you have done to my with your love.

I truly hope that your English level now would be much better so you can understand my words. I'm sorry for not being a good teacher with strict discipline and helped you improving language skills.

So here it goes ...

Thank you for loving me

Your love was the most truthful thing in this world. You came to me with a sincere heart, no lie, no cheat, nothing to hide. You shared with me your life and everything's right. Your eyes were only looking at me, your hands were always holding mine, and your arms were always hugging me. You showed me what real love should be and gave me everything you had even when you had nothing at all. You make me feel like I'm the only girl in this world, always being there to help me out of the trouble I made, always let me do whatever I want. You really loved me for who I truly am. 

You loved me the best when I am a mess. You told me how beautiful I am, and made me feel it. Inside your warm arms, I did not have to give a damn about the world outside, and all my worries suddenly went away. Under your hot lips, I could let go of my social mask, stayed true to myself and embraced every heart beat, I laughed more than ever being with you, and I cried more than ever away from you. I am at my best, my happiest by your side.

Yes, I was careless, I was unaware of what I had. I did not know how to love back then, did not know how to kiss or how to hold you closer so you won't let me go. If only I was more thoughtful and stronger to fight the world for you and for us. I could not help you with your life. I could not be there with you through hell. I was so small to protect you from the harshness of life. I'm sorry\!

I have always wonder if there were not so many differences in our lives, would you still be by my side? 'Cause there's nothing different between us or our connected souls.

If I tell you that I am now hurt enough to know how to treasure your love, will you keep hiding in the past, or will you come back in my life?

I miss you, a lot\! And love you still\!

I know that I should move on with my life, that I will soon go to a far away land, and you cannot come with me. But I still remember you once said that you will try to go and visit me, no matter what corner of the world I am in. Will you keep that promise and let me see you again?
