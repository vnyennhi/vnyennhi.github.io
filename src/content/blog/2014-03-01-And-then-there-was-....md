I was young and naive
I was lost and carefree
Just little me


And then there was you


You was old and wise
You was high and careless
Just too much you


And then there was us


We were true and happy
We were bonded and broken
Just love


But then there'll be me


I am young and naive
I am lost and messy
Just too much hurt


But then there'll be none


...
'cause you are the only exception \(Paramore\)
