"There" is the place I don't wanna leave.
"Back" is the time I don't wanna pass.
"Here" is the place I don't wanna live.
"Then" is the time I don't wanna come.
Back there, I met You.
Then here, I see You.
"Here" & "There" \- just one place.
"Back" & "Then" \- just one time.
A position \- every movement stops.
A moment \- every motion stops.
"You" \- is "there" & "back"
"I" \- is "here" & "then"
"You" & "I" \- just one.
A person \- every emotion stops.
Where is "here" & "there"?
When is "back" & "then"?
The answer is up there.
Don't want to look back?
Then, it's down here:
"There" is the place You don't wanna pass.
"Back" is the time You don't wanna leave.
"Here" is the place You don't wanna come.
"Then" is the time You don't wanna live.
How come back then, You & I are here & there?
The answer is up there.
Don't want to look back?
Then, it's down here:
"You" & "I" \- just one.
You & I can't be here & there at different back & then.
\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-
What if ...
"Here" not be "There" ?
What if ...
"Then" not be "Back" ?
So could ...
"You" not be "I" ?
