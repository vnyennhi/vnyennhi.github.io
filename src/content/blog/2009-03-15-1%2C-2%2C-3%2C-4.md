One, for the sake of one,
Two, for the love of you,
Three, times hurt,
Four your ignorance,


There is only
One way
Two express
Three words
I love you


It's what I'll do
When I'm ready
When you're ready
When the time is right
That's what I'll do


I'll ask you
For words
That tell you
Three words


To be true
I love you


Just reply
In one of
Two ways,
There is no third,
Just yes or no,
No maybes;
They aren't a choice


Yes you will,
No you won't


Only one question


Only two answers


Only three words


Only for you


I love you


When I'm ready
When I think you are, too
When the time is right
We will see
What it'll be


There's only one thing 


to do


Three words 


for you 


I love you


There's only one way 


to say


Those three words 


and that's what I'll do, 


to be true


I love you


Once, I needed you,
Twice, more than you needed me,
Thrice, times I died,
Four my Feelings,


So 1,2,3,4
Close your eyes
Your dream will be mine
No big surprise
You’ll no longer be weary


Only one person


To make my heart shaken


Three wishes I was granted


All meant for you


So One time I loved you,
Two times I lost you,
Third times I left,
But forever returned 
For you


'cause not only one time


two times


those three words


all time I say


I Love You
