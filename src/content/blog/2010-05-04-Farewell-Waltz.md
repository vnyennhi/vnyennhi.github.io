One last waltz
Sunk by rhythms
Sucked by soul
Saddened by fear
And sobered by tears

One last waltz
A single sway
A solo swing
A siren scare
And a solemn swear

Set me free
To fall at last
Hold me back
To whisper fast

Too late
Just a little too late
To know
Too
Sweet temptation is
Too
Sinful seduction is
Too
Sacred salvation is
Too much
Just a little too much
To cry

One last waltz
One last touch
One last hug
One last kiss
One last …
Just once

It’s for real
Our one last waltz
Just one last waltz
The farewell waltz

04.05.10

\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-
Chopin \- Waltz in B minor Op.69 No.2
Better known as "The farewell waltz"[mediafire.com/?5menzhg5n5i](http://www.mediafire.com/?5menzhg5n5i)
I actually like this version best among all, not too slow, not too fast, 
just too enough to capture my heart. 

I wrote the poem above under the inspiration of this miraculously splendid piano piece by the one and only Chopin.
