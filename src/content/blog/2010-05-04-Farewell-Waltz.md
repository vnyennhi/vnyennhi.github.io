One last waltz
Sunk by rhythms
Sucked by soul
Saddened by fear
And sobered by tears


One last waltz
A single sway
A solo swing
A siren scare
And a solemn swear


Set me free
To fall at last
Hold me back
To whisper fast


Too late
Just a little too late
To know
Too
Sweet temptation is
Too
Sinful seduction is
Too
Sacred salvation is
Too much
Just a little too much
To cry


One last waltz
One last touch
One last hug
One last kiss
One last …
Just once


It’s for real
Our one last waltz
Just one last waltz
The farewell waltz


04.05.10
