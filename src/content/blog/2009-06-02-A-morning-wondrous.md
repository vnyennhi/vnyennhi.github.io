Morning sunlight flooded through branches
A curtain of crystal drops shed with ease
Forever be apart from trees
Dazzling halos sheltered the pure
Trying to flee in a summer breeze
Memory was not meant to be
All forgotten in an early rush
Frigid wind waltzing dawn chorus
Could not hold a salute brush
Could not grasp a solemn hush
The pass\-by lingered not but dusk
However lost in a beauteous
A smile with blush

\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-
This poem is for a very beautiful view I saw this morning, at Vo Van Tan & Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Crossroad. It was a natural 1\-min miracle that helped me relieve my anxious about the noon Macroeconomic Mid\-exam. 
A morning sweet smile for me I guess ...
