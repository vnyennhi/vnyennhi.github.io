On the way back home
Under heavy rain
I just move along with you without a siren call

There's a hush between us that cant' be filled
I wonder whether if I should rush to you without a sign

Please tell me that you feel the same
We are meant to be together until forever

I cant' hide my love
All my true feeling
For all this time I wait till now
I long to tell you my ...

Confession.
