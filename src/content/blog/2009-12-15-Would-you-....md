Would you shed me a drop of tear
When I throw myself off
Would you confront the fear
To pull me back when the time is near


I want to cry
But the tear wouldn't come
I want to jump
But the time wouldn't come


Would you lend me your shoulder
Would you lend me your open arms
For I need a place to hide away
For I need a hug to feel a little warm


Shelter me now
Would you?
Hide me now
Would you?
Hold me now
Would you?
Save me now
Would you?


Would you take me in
Would you keep me close
Would you hold me tight
And never let go?
Would you smile me on
Would you cry me off
Would you live me love
And always lie to ...


... me


For me


Would you?


\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-
Written in a desperately unbalancing condition of inability to shed a drop of tear.


\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-
Feeling indeed hurt and really need a hug of specific person despite all the impossibility.


\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-\-
Unable to figure out the ground or find the way out for this so is trying to bear the bitter vestibular disorder
