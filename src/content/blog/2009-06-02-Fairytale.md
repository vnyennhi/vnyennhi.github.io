In a land far, far away


There is the land of fairy tales.


The hero will always win.


The villain will always lose.


And lovers will always be close.


This is the natural law of this land.
However, sometimes, just sometimes,


This law is never broken, but bent.


The story does not always go as planned.
This is the tale of one not go as planned


---


Years ago, when I was younger
I kinda liked a girl I knew
She was mine and we were sweethearts
That was then, but then it's true


---


She was walking by


Gently left no fragrance behind


She never smiled. She never cried,


She never laughed. She only sighed


And continued with her own glide.


---
I'm in love with a fairytale


Even though it hurts
'Cause I don't care if I lose my mind
I'm already cursed


---


You told me once. You told me twice


You told me for most of my life


Never love one not your kind


Never fall for a siren cry


---
Every day we started fighting
Every night we fell in love
No one else could make me sadder
But no one else could lift me high above


---


Is it over yet?


Yes, it is over yet.


The fragile tears cover sadness


Could not return to where had left


For her it shed


---


She's a fairytale, yeah...
Even though it hurts
'Cause I don't care if I lose my mind
I'm already cursed


---


This poem is written down in the inspiration by the song "Fairytale" \- 1st prize at Eurovision song contest. Mostly of the story is my delusional dream ^^
